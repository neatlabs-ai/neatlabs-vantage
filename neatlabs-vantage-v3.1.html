Turn Your LinkedIn Feed Into a Threat Intel Machine

Most people follow hundreds of accounts and get noise. This guide shows you how to use a curated expert feed as an active intelligence asset — not just a news scroll. Whether you're a SOC analyst, CISO, compliance professional, or consultant, a properly curated feed changes how you work.

Daily Routines
☀️
10-Minute Morning Brief
Daily Routine
Treat your curated feed as your morning intelligence brief. You're scanning for signals, not scrolling for entertainment.
Sort by "Most recent" — LinkedIn's algorithm buries time-sensitive posts
Flag posts referencing active threat actors, new CVEs, or policy changes
Note when multiple experts converge on the same incident — that's signal
🔔
Notifications for Top Tier
Alert Strategy
Reserve LinkedIn post notifications for your top 5–10 highest-signal follows: researchers who break news, not commentators who react.
Bell icon on their profile — instant push notification when they post
Creates a "tier 1 alert layer" inside LinkedIn — no extra tools needed
📌
Build a Tiered Watch List
Focus System
Not all follows are equal. Tier 1: active researchers (check daily). Tier 2: strategic leaders (weekly). Tier 3: policy voices (monthly).
Export your starred list from this tool and annotate with tiers
Quality over volume — 20 excellent follows beats 200 mediocre ones

Daily Workflow
⏱️ A Realistic 15-Minute Daily Intelligence Routine
Step 01 · Morning
Scan Your Feed
5 min. Switch to "Most Recent." Scan Tier 1 follows. Flag anything referencing active campaigns, CVEs, or policy changes.
Step 02 · Mid-Morning
Read Flagged Posts
5 min. Open saved posts. Ask: does this change anything I'm monitoring? Does a client need to know today?
Step 03 · Midday
Engage or Share
2 min. Add a substantive comment or share with context. Builds presence without creating original content daily.
Step 04 · Weekly
Synthesize & Write
3 min/day amortized. Turn the week's themes into a short post or internal briefing. Thought leadership compounds here.
Step 05 · Monthly
Audit Your Follows
Check for signal degradation. Unfollow anyone who's gone quiet, shifted topics, or is posting noise. Quality > volume.
💡
The compounding effect: After 90 days, your LinkedIn algorithm learns what you care about and starts surfacing more of it. Your feed becomes self-reinforcing — higher signal, less noise, more relevant connections finding you. The best threat intelligence isn't a product you subscribe to. It's a network you cultivate.
